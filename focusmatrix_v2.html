The 3 Competitive Moats
01
Data Moat — Switching Cost Grows With Time
Every week of use builds a personalized behavioral baseline. Your 12-week productivity history isn't transferable to Todoist. The longer someone uses FocusMatrix, the more irreplaceable it becomes — exactly like Spotify's "Wrapped" creates lock-in through personalized data.
02
Framework Lock-In — Already Trusted by Millions
The Eisenhower Matrix is taught in every MBA program, leadership curriculum, and business book. Stephen Covey, Tim Ferriss, and hundreds of productivity coaches already pre-sell this framework. FocusMatrix is the first tool to operationalize it with real behavioral data — so our distribution is already built into the culture.
03
B2B Expansion Path — From Personal to Enterprise
Individual users who prove personal ROI become champions inside their companies. Aggregated (anonymized) team analytics — showing whether a team spends time on strategic work — is a $10K+/year enterprise product. No other productivity tool has this behavioral intelligence layer ready to sell.
